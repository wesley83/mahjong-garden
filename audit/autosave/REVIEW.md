| AUTO-QUEUE | Version 1 names, pending legacy payload and accepted keys migrate to version 2 without changing their values. | AUTO-RECOVERY | Serialized fresh read-modify-write and revision-checked IndexedDB commits precede network activity. Quota/conflict never permits an uncommitted send. Offline failure stops the drain, retaining the queue; Retry/online resumes. Lost response reconciles before another POST. A frozen completion survives leaving during delayed preparation; late sending cannot replace/reopen newer gameplay. |
| AUTO-PUBLISH | New outcomes join the shared release gate and its refusal tests. Exact source/candidate receipts, independent native worker journey, source provenance, pushed clean commit, fresh verified backup, conditional promotion and actual public update/checkpoint verification remain required. No wipe/schema/score-service maintenance. |
